Free add-on for Google Sheets™

Predict the blanks in your sheet.

Pick a column such as Converted or Churned. Predit learns from the rows where you know the answer and predicts it for every row where it is still blank, with a chance, a rank, and one plain sentence on how far to trust it.

A list you can work down

Every blank row gets a chance and a rank, so your team can start with the leads most likely to say yes.

An honest trust score

Predit first tests itself on rows where you already know the answer and tells you, in one sentence, how well it did. If the result is poor or suspiciously perfect, it asks before writing anything.

Leak checks

Columns that give the answer away, such as a field filled in only after a deal closes, are left out, so the score isn't flattering by accident.

How it works

  1. Open Predit

    In your spreadsheet choose Extensions → Predit → Start. A sidebar opens on the right.

  2. Pick the column

    Choose the column with the answers you know, for example Converted. Predit shows how many rows it will fill in and where the new columns will go.

  3. Click Predict

    Predit checks itself, fills in the blank rows and shows the trust score and the most likely rows. Undo removes what it added.

The first run downloads the model once, about 85 MB. Later runs start from your browser's cache. Read the getting-started guide.

Where your data goes

Predit has no server of its own. Your spreadsheet is read and written by Predit's Apps Script code, which runs on Google's servers under your account, and the predictions are computed in your browser. Your sheet data only travels between Google and your browser. No request to anyone else contains it, and we never receive it.

Predit asks Google for only two permissions: to see and edit the spreadsheet you use it in, and to show its sidebar. It cannot see your other files, your email or your contacts.

Predit's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What you need

Google Sheets™ on a computer

Add-on sidebars are available in Sheets in a desktop browser.

A current browser

Chrome or Edge with WebGPU is fastest. Other browsers work too, more slowly.

Some known answers

At least 20 rows with the answer filled in. A few hundred make the trust score much more reliable.
