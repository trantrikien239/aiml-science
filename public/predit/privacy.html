Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026. This policy covers the Predit add-on for Google Sheets™ ("Predit") and this website.

In short. Predit reads and writes only the spreadsheet you use it in, and computes its predictions in your own browser. Your sheet data travels only between Google and your browser: no request to anyone else contains it, and we never receive it. Predit has no accounts, no analytics and no server of its own, and it does not ask for your email address.

4. How your spreadsheet data is processed

  • Reading. When you open the sidebar and start a prediction, Predit's Apps Script code reads the open sheet and passes the values to the sidebar over Google's own connection.
  • Predicting. The model runs inside the sidebar, on your computer. The rows are held in your browser's memory for the run and are discarded when you close the sidebar. They are never written to browser storage and never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Writing. Predit writes its result columns into the same sheet, only on rows where the column you chose is blank. If its own check says the results are not reliable or look too good to be true, it asks you first. With Also fill the blanks unticked, it changes none of your cells. Only if you tick it does it also write its predicted answer into the column you chose, and then only into its empty cells; later it updates or removes only its own guesses there. Before writing it checks that the rows have not been sorted, moved or edited since they were read.

5. Every network request Predit makes

Apart from Google's own traffic between Google Sheets and your browser, the Predit sidebar makes only these requests. None of them contains spreadsheet data, your email address or any identifier of your Google account.

RequestToWhen
Google's standard stylesheet for add-onsssl.gstatic.com (Google)Each time the sidebar opens
ONNX Runtime Web 1.31.1, the open-source engine that runs the model (3 files, about 29 MB)cdn.jsdelivr.net, a public content delivery networkOnce; afterwards from your browser's cache
The TabICLv2 model, converted to the ONNX format (2 files, about 56 MB)huggingface.co (Hugging Face, Inc.)Once; afterwards from your browser's cache
This website (Help or Privacy link, opened in a new tab)aiml.science/predit, hosted on GitHub PagesOnly when you click such a link

Like any web server, these hosts see your IP address, your browser type and the address of the page that asked (a Google domain). Every downloaded program file is checked against a fixed SHA-256 fingerprint before it is used. If we move these downloads to a host of our own, we will update this section before the change.

Predit's Apps Script code makes no outside requests at all: its permissions don't allow it.

7. What we never do

  • We never receive, sell, rent or share your spreadsheet data, and we never use any data for advertising.
  • No human at Predit reads your spreadsheet data; we never receive it. If you choose to send us part of a sheet in a support request, we use it only to answer that request.
  • We never use your data to create, train or improve any machine-learning or AI model. Predit's model is pre-trained and fixed. Your rows act only as examples for your own run, in your browser, and are discarded afterwards.
  • Predit contains no analytics, advertising or error-reporting code.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Predit's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Predit's use of Google Workspace data also follows the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy: the data is used only to provide the predictions you ask for, it is not transferred to anyone, and no one reads it.

10. Security

  • All connections use HTTPS.
  • The model files and the ONNX Runtime program files are checked against fixed SHA-256 fingerprints before use; a file that doesn't match is never run.
  • Predit's code is not obfuscated.

11. Your rights and choices

  • Stop Predit's access: uninstall it in Google Sheets (Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons, then Options → Uninstall), and remove its access in your Google Account's third-party connections.
  • Remove Predit's columns: click Remove Predit's columns in the sidebar right after a run, or delete the columns whose header starts with "Predit:".
  • Clear downloaded files: clear site data for googleusercontent.com in your browser settings.
  • Questions or requests about your data (for example about a support email you sent us): write to trantrikien239@gmail.com. We'll answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority. In the EU or the UK, that is the authority of the country where you live.

We don't sell or share personal information, and the only personal data we handle is what you put in a support email. We answer those emails because you asked us to (in the EEA and UK: our legitimate interest in helping users).
