3. What Predit does

Predit predicts the values of a column you choose for rows where that column is blank. It writes a chance (as a percentage or as High/Medium/Low) and a rank, plus the predicted answer when the column has more than two possible answers, and it reports a trust score based on a check against rows whose answers you already know. The model runs in your browser. How data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.

4. Predictions are estimates

  • Predictions and trust scores are statistical estimates based on the data in your sheet. They can be wrong, and the trust score itself is uncertain, especially with few rows.
